# Leave No Trace — 7 Principles (reference)

1. **Plan ahead and prepare** — know regulations, weather, and your limits.
2. **Travel on durable surfaces** — stay on trail; camp on rock, gravel, or dry grass.
3. **Dispose of waste properly** — pack it in, pack it out; catholes 6-8 inches deep, 200 ft from water.
4. **Leave what you find** — don't move rocks, pick protected plants, or carve trees.
5. **Minimize campfire impact** — use a stove; keep fires small, use established rings, burn wood to ash.
6. **Respect wildlife** — observe from distance; never feed animals; store food securely.
7. **Be considerate of others** — yield the trail, keep noise down, take breaks off-trail.
